# Free subscriptions — sign up to these first

Everything here is **free** or **free with email signup** (no card). Work down the list.
"Confirmed" = I verified the page exists. "Verify" = go to the site and find the current
sign-up/RSS link yourself before relying on it.

---

## Tier 1 — Genuinely free, no account (do these first)

### ASIC — media releases  ✅ Confirmed
- Sign up: https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/sign-up-for-news-alerts
- How: enter an email (an alias is fine), pick media releases. Done.
- Flag: 🔴 ACT. Never AI-only — read at source.

### AFCA — published determinations  ✅ Confirmed
- Search page: https://www.afca.org.au/what-to-expect/search-published-decisions
- Datacube (complaints data): https://data.afca.org.au
- How: no signup to read. Follow @AFCA_org_au for update pings, or check weekly.
- Note: AFCA lets you reproduce de-identified determinations if unaltered + credited (non-commercial).
- Flag: 🔴 ACT.

### ABS — economic data  ✅ Confirmed (verify exact alert page)
- Site: https://www.abs.gov.au  (look for "Release calendar" + email notifications)
- How: subscribe to release notifications for the series you care about (CPI, wages).
- Flag: 🟢 NOTE.

### Treasury — consultations  ✅ Confirmed (verify exact alert page)
- Site: https://treasury.gov.au/consultation
- How: check the consultation hub; subscribe to updates if offered.
- Flag: 🟢 NOTE.

## How to actually "download" these into your tool

You don't scrape any of these. Two legitimate intake routes:

1. **RSS** — if a source publishes a feed, your script reads it directly (see the code).
   Many of the above have one; confirm the feed URL on each site (look for the RSS icon,
   or try adding /feed or /rss to the news page, or check the page source).

2. **Email** — for newsletters, send them to a dedicated folder/label. (A Gmail label +
   filter is the simplest. Reading email into the tool is a later step; start with RSS.)

Start with ASIC + AFCA + one trade newsletter. Add more once the loop works.
