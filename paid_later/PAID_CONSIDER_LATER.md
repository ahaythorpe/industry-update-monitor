# Paid — consider later, when there's budget

None of these are needed to stay current now. They buy depth and time, not basic awareness.
Revisit when you're advising and need this on tap. Verify current prices yourself —
I haven't confirmed them and they change.

| Source | What the paid tier adds | Why wait |
|---|---|---|
| Financial Standard (full) | Journal archive, deeper analysis, some events | Free FS Advice newsletter covers current news |
| AFR (Australian Financial Review) | Broad business/finance journalism, breaking markets | Free primary sources + trade newsletters cover the advice-specific line |
| Morningstar Australia | Investment research, fund/stock data | Not needed for regulation/compliance learning |
| Firstlinks (free, but premium ecosystem) | Deeper wealth/super commentary | Free tier already strong |
| Deep technical platforms (e.g. provider research tools) | On-tap technical rulings for live advice | Only valuable once advising clients |

## Rule for upgrading
Only pay when a *specific recurring need* isn't met by the free layer —
e.g. "I keep needing the full article, not the summary" or "I need historical data the
free tier won't show." Pay for the gap you actually hit, not in advance.

## Audio and podcasts — optional, free-first
Audio is useful for background learning, but it is not part of the monitor's evidence
pipeline. Listen in a normal podcast app and verify anything important against the linked
show notes or a primary source.

Good free candidates to keep in the listening list:

| Source | Access | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| The ifa Show | Free podcast feed/app | Advice-industry interviews and discussion |
| SMSF Adviser Show | Free podcast feed/app | SMSF and superannuation discussion |
| Official regulator or professional-body podcasts | Usually free feed/app | Primary or near-primary context; verify the publisher |
| Publisher show notes and transcripts | Free where provided | Searchable companion material, without downloading protected audio |

Spotify can be a listening app, but its API should not be treated as a transcript or audio
source. Do not scrape Spotify, download protected episodes, or obtain transcripts through an
unapproved route. A future summariser should accept only a public transcript, official show
notes, or audio the user is legitimately allowed to process.

### Audio cost decision

Start with manual listening and free show notes. If summaries become worthwhile, measure one
week first: speech-to-text plus summarisation can cost more than text-feed summaries, and
pricing/limits change. Use a prepaid API balance and hard spend cap, batch one weekly job,
process only selected episodes, store short summaries plus the original link, and skip audio
when an official transcript or detailed show notes exist. Keep podcast summaries as KNOW/NOTE
material; an ACT conclusion still requires reading the primary source.
